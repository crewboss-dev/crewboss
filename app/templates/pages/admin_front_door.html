Get a padlock for your own web address: no browser warning, and it unlocks passwordless sign-in with a passkey.

Three steps. Your network address keeps working alongside it.

{% if can_change %}

1. Your web address

Don't have one? A free one takes two minutes
  1. Go to duckdns.org and sign in.
  2. Pick a name, for example myteam. You get myteam.duckdns.org.
  3. Put your internet connection's address in the "current ip" box and press "update ip".

Keep the name for good: passkeys are tied to it.

2. Point it here and open port 80

At your web address provider, point the address at your internet connection's address. On your router, forward port 80 (and 443) to this computer, {{ this_computer }}.

Where do I find that on my router?

Look for a menu called Port Forwarding, Virtual Server or NAT. Add a rule for port 80 and another for 443, both to {{ this_computer }}. It is often easier if the router always gives this computer the same address (a "DHCP reservation").

Some routers can't open their own public address from inside the network. That is why the network address keeps working.
